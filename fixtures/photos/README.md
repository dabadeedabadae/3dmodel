# Fixture photos

Source: **Clothing dataset** by Alexey Grigorev — https://github.com/alexeygrigorev/clothing-dataset — licence **CC0 1.0** (`LICENSE-CC0.txt`), "can be freely used for any purpose, including commercial".
Files are named `<dataset label>_<first 8 chars of the dataset image id>.jpg`; the label is the dataset's own label (not ours). 26 photos picked with a fixed random seed (7): 14 T-Shirt, 3 Pants, 3 Skirt, 2 Shoes, 2 Dress, 2 Longsleeve; `kids=False` only. Resolution is the repository's 400 px version.

They are real flat-lay photos taken by many different people — contrasting and busy backgrounds, shadows, folds — i.e. exactly the input this module is for. Nothing here is curated to make the models look good.
